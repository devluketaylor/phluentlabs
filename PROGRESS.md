- **Fix (idempotent work-list model):** (1) pre-create a `queued` recipient row per audience member with `onConflictDoNothing` on the existing unique `(newsletter_id, subscriber_id)` index → durable work-list; (2) only email recipients whose status is NOT `sent` → re-runs skip already-sent people and auto-retry `queued`/`failed`; (3) `sendBatchWithRetry` — bounded exponential backoff (3 attempts, 500ms→1s) on TRANSIENT errors only (429/5xx/network, incl. the SDK's `{data:null,error}` shape); permanent 4xx not retried; (4) a batch still failing after retries marks JUST its recipients `failed` with the error message and continues with remaining batches (no whole-issue abort); (5) the issue is finalized `sent` only when zero recipients remain non-sent, otherwise restored to its original status (`scheduled` issues stay scheduled so the cron re-runs and retries only the failed rows; the scheduled-send cron/tRPC/`sendViaApiKey` are unchanged and benefit automatically).
- **Return type** extended `{sent}` → `{sent, failed, alreadySent}`; all three callers (`newsletter.send`, `sendScheduledDue`, `sendViaApiKey`, cron route) destructure `{sent}` and remain compatible.
